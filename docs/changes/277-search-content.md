# 277 Search this app's records from the top bar

- Backend: `GET /api/search?q=` (`backend/src/search.ts`) follows the shared shape in project-standards `docs/directory-rules.md` ("Content search"): `q` 1 to 100 characters, answers `{ results }` of `{ title, href, kind: 'content', snippet? }`, at most 20 records, newest first. It searches items (title, subtype, review, tags, companions, details), essays (title, body, tags), day log entries (title, body, date) and imported documents (title, body; deleted ones left out). Every word must appear, ignoring case; `\`, `%` and `_` match literally. It sits behind the shared sign-in like the other routes and is listed in `openapi.ts` and `docs/openapi.json`.
- Screen: `frontend/src/search-content.ts` calls the endpoint with the dialog's abort signal and is passed to `AppShell` as `searchContent` in `frontend/src/app.tsx`. A result opens its screen: `/essays/:id`, `/journal/<date>`, `/documents/:id`. Items have no screen of their own, so an item opens the items table filtered to its title (`/table?q=<title>`).
- Tests: `backend/test/search.test.ts` (refused queries touch no database, word patterns, row mapping), `backend/test/search.integration.test.ts` (real tables; runs where `TEST_DATABASE_URL` is set), `frontend/src/search.test.tsx` (type a word, the record shows, a click opens it), and the `/api/search` mount in `backend/test/openapi.test.ts`.
- Limits: the integration test did not run on the author's PC (no database); CI runs it.
